Bio

After several master’s degrees and a few years in tech, I started looking for conversations with phantoms: in light and shadow, in music, in human emotions and resonances, in everything that appears beyond the object. What’s left (after) everything.

I also collect Belgian vintage fashion and enjoy visual experiments.

Based in Paris, born in a place you’ve probably never heard of.
